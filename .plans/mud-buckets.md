# MUD rollout buckets

Built from `tmp/survey/all.json` (497 water districts, W1–W55) by `scripts/build-mud-buckets.ts`, per `.plans/mud-scrapers-rollout-spec.md` §3. Vendor keys come from `vendorKey()` in `lib/ingest/siteSurvey.ts`: board platform, then footer credit, then off-site agenda host, then custom theme, then Drive plus builder, then builder. Generic builder keys (wordpress, wix, …) group sites that only share a builder, not a firm; the bucket's worker confirms whether one adapter fits them all. `listing:` means the self-reported website is a tax office's or law firm's page for the district. `unknown` is mostly sites whose fetch failed during the survey (retry first). The spec's 498 counts a blank, unnamed entry at the end of W48 in `district-rollout-batches.md`; it has no name, so no ID and no bucket here.
