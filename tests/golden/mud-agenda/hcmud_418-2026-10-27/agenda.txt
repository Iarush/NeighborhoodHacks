HARRIS COUNTY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 418
NOTICE OF PUBLIC MEETING
Notice is hereby given to all interested members of the public that the Board of Directors
of the above-captioned District will hold a regular meeting at 1300 Post Oak Boulevard, Suite
2500, Houston, Texas 77056, said address being a meeting place of the District.
The meeting will be held on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at 11:00 a.m.
The Board shall consider and discuss the following matters and take any action appropriate
with respect to such matters:
1. Public comments;
2. Approval of the minutes of the meeting of the Board of Directors held on September 22,
2026;
3. Bookkeeper's Report, including (i) financial and investment reports, and authorizing the
payment of invoices presented, and (ii) status of compliance by the District with Texas
Government Code, Chapter 2265, relative to energy reporting;
4. Annual review of Order Establishing Policy for Investment of District Funds and
appointing Investment Officer, and adoption of Resolution in connection therewith;
5. Adoption of Resolution Adopting List of Qualified Brokers Authorized to Engage in
Investment Transactions with the District;
6. Preparation and filing of continuing disclosure report for fiscal year ended May 31, 2026
(due November 30, 2026);
7. Receive Tax Assessor-Collector report and approve payment of invoices presented and
moving of accounts to uncollectible roll;
8. Report on legal action taken by the District's delinquent tax collections attorney, authorize
foreclosure proceedings, installment agreements, and the filing of proofs of claim;
9. Conduct a public hearing regarding the adoption of the District's 2026 tax rate;
10. Levy of the District's 2026 maintenance tax rate, and the adoption of an Order in connection
therewith;
11. Approval and execution of an Amendment to the Seventh Amended and Restated District
Information Form;
