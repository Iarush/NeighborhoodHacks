NOTICE OF MEETING
HARRIS COUNTY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 383
The Board of Directors of Harris County Municipal Utility District No. 383 will
hold a regular meeting on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at 12:00 p.m., at the offices of
Municipal Accounts & Consulting, L.P., 1281 Brittmoore Road, Houston, Texas 77043, to
discuss and, if appropriate, act upon the following items:
1. Receive comments from the public.
2. CONSENT AGENDA: The following items are considered routine by the Board
and will be enacted by one motion. No separate discussion will occur on these
items unless a Board member so requests, in which event the item will be
removed and placed on the Non-Consent Agenda.
a. Approve minutes.
b. Development in the District and elsewhere in Gleannloch Farms.
c. Tax assessment and collection matters, including delinquent tax collections
and payment of tax bills.
d. Review Arbitrage Rebate Report for the Series 2016A Refunding Bonds.
e. Financial and bookkeeping matters, including payment of bills and review
of investments.
i. conduct annual review of Investment Policy and adopt Resolution
Regarding Annual Review of Investment Policy; and
ii. review, revise and adopt Resolution Establishing the Authorized
Depository Institutions and Adopting List of Qualified
Broker/Dealers with Whom the District May Engage in Investment
Transactions.
f. Garbage and recycling matters.
g. Operation of District facilities, including:
i. billings, repairs and maintenance, customer correspondence, and
message language on water bills; and
ii. sanitary sewer video inspection program and repairs.
h. District park matters, community improvement projects, and related
coordination with community association.
i. Channel and pond maintenance, including detention and drainage facilities
report.
j. District website and communication matters.
3. NON-CONSENT AGENDA
a. Items removed from Consent Agenda.
b. Additional tax matters, including:
i. conduct public hearing regarding District's tax rate, adopt Order
Levying Taxes, and authorize execution of Amendment to
Information Form.
