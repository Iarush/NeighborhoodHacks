HARRIS COUNTY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 565
Notice is hereby given pursuant to V.T.C.A. Government Code, Chapter 551, that the Board of
Directors of Harris County Municipal Utility District No. 565 will meet in regular session, open to the
public, at 12:00 pm on Thursday, October 15, 2026, at 9 Greenway Plaza, Ste. 1000, Houston, Texas,
77046, a designated meeting location outside the boundaries of the District. At the meeting the following
items will be considered and acted on:
1. Hear from the public.
2. Conduct public hearing on proposed 2026 tax rate.
3. Adopt Order Setting Tax Rate.
4. Approve Amended Notice to Sellers and Purchasers.
5. Consider approving minutes of September 17, 2026 Board of Directors Meeting.
6. Hear NJ Services’ Report and consider acting thereon, including:
a. Discuss and ratify repair/landscape proposals and take appropriate action.
7. Hear Bookkeeper’s Report and consider acting thereon, including:
a. Approve payment of District bills presented and the investment report.
8. Hear Tax Assessor-Collector’s Report and consider acting thereon, including:
a. Authorize payments from tax account;
b. Discuss delinquent accounts, consider taking action to terminate service to delinquent
taxpayers;
c. Consider approving payment agreements for delinquent tax accounts; and
d. Approve B&A Assignment to Assessments of the Southwest.
9. Hear Operator’s Report and consider acting thereon, including:
a. Authorize repair and maintenance of District’s facilities; and
b. Authorize termination of water service to delinquent accounts.
10. Hear Engineer’s Report and consider acting thereon, including:
a. Authorize the design of District facilities;
b. Approve plans and specifications for construction of District facilities;
c. Authorize advertisement for bids for District projects;
d. Approve pay estimates and change orders for construction projects in the District; and
e. Authorize and/or ratify construction contracts and related items.
11. Hear Developer’s Report and consider acting thereon.
12. Hear Attorney’s Report and consider acting thereon.
Pursuant to V.T.C.A., Government Code, Chapter 551, the Board of Directors may convene in closed
session to receive advice of legal counsel, discuss matters relating to pending or contemplated litigation, personnel
matters, real estate transactions, gifts and donations, security devices and/or economic development negotiations.
Electronic copies of the meeting materials are available by contacting mbritton@coatsrose.com. Persons with
disabilities who plan to attend this meeting and would like to request auxiliary aids or services are
requested to contact the District's attorney at 1 (713) 651-0111 at least three business days prior to the meeting so
that appropriate arrangements can be made.
EXECUTED this 9th day of October, 2026.
