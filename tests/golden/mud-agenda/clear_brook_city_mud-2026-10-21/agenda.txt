CLEAR BROOK CITY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT
N O T I C E
In accordance with Chapter 551.125, Texas Government Code, take notice that the Board of Directors of Clear
Brook City Municipal Utility District will meet in regular session at 7:30 p.m. on October 21, 2026. The meeting will
be open to the public at the following location: 11911 Blackhawk, Houston, Harris County, Texas, a meeting place of
the District. Directors, consultants, and the general public may participate in the meeting by attending the meeting at the
location noted above. At the meeting, the Board will consider the following:
1. Invocation;
2. Public Hearing on 2026 Tax Rate;
3. Close Public Hearing;
4. Adopt Order Setting Tax Rate and Levying Tax for 2026;
5. Approve Amended District Information Form;
6. Public comments (3 minutes);
7. Approve minutes of the meetings on September 16, 2026;
8. Review and approve Amended District’s Disaster Recovery Plan, including updated of Annual Testing of this
Plan;
9. Review and approve Tax Assessor and Collector’s Report, including status of delinquent tax accounts and
authorizing the payment of invoices presented, approving tax refunds, and approve moving of accounts to
uncollectible roll;
10. Review and approve Bookkeeper’s Report, payment of District bills, and monthly check register;
11. Review and approve Engineer’s Report take any necessary action concerning on-going construction within the
District, consider pay estimates, authorize additional construction bid advertisement if necessary, and if timely,
award contracts on additional District improvements, consider any change orders, consider capacity
commitments; consider approval of job assignment proposal(s); discuss engineering services, and take any
necessary action in connection with engineering matters;
12. Developer’s Report;
13. Review and approve Amended Professional Engineering Service Agreement;
14. Review and approve Administrative Report;
15. Review and Approve Operator’s Report and authorization of any necessary maintenance;
Director-Requested Items
16. Review and approve a survey to be distributed for District residents (Director Rabe);
17. Discuss household hazardous waste collection throughout the District and take any necessary actions (Director
Rabe);
18. Discuss and take action regarding procedures for public comments, including designation of a timekeeper
(Director Rabe);
19. Consider and discuss letter from Sageglen HOA to the CBC board of directors related to director behavior and
participation in HOA meetings and take any necessary action (Director Clark);
20. Pending business;
