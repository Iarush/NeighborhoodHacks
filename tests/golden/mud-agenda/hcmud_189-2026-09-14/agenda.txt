HARRIS COUNTY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 189
N O T I C E
In accordance with chapter 551, Texas Government Code, and Section 49.063, Texas Water
Code, both as amended, take notice that the Board of Directors of Harris County Municipal
Utility District No. 189 will meet at Norton Rose Fulbright Tower, 1550 Lamar Street, Suite 2000,
Houston, Texas 77010, and via teleconference at 12:00 p.m. on Monday, September 14, 2026.
Meeting documents will be made available at https://www.hcmud189.org/meetings/agenda-
packets/.
To join the meeting via teleconference, please use the dial instructions below:

At such meeting, the Board will consider and act on the following matters:
1. Public comments;
2. Review and approve minutes of the regular meeting of August 3, 2026;
3. Report in connection with law enforcement agreement and any action regarding District
security;
4. Discuss and consider Energy Physical presentation, and take any necessary action;
5. Renew District Insurance;
6. Authorize Indemnification of Directors and take any necessary action;
7. Receive and approve Tax Assessor and Collector’s Report, including payment of bills;
8. Discuss tax rate recommendation with Financial Advisor;
9. Adopt Order Designating Officer to Calculate and Publish Tax Rate;
10. Receive and approve Bookkeeper’s Report, payment of certain bills, and investment
report;
11. Review Engineer’s Report, including approval of pay estimates/change orders, approval
of capacity commitment requests, and authorizing necessary action related to plans;
i. Discuss request for annexation;
12. Discuss and consider amending the District Rate Order, and take any necessary action;
13. Review and approve Operator’s Report, including approval of accounts to be written off,
approval of hydrant inspections, and necessary action relating to District’s facilities;
14. Report from Ethoscapes regarding detention ponds, and take any necessary action;
15. Discuss and consider proposal for additional mowing at Darbydale Pond, and take any
necessary action;
16. Review monthly website analytics;
and any matters that may properly come before the Board.
