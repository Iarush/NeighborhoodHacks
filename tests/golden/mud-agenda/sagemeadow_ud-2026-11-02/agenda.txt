NOTICE OF MEETING
SAGEMEADOW UTILITY DISTRICT
OF HARRIS COUNTY, TEXAS
TO: THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF SAGEMEADOW UTILITY DISTRICT OF HARRIS COUNTY,
TEXAS, AND TO ALL OTHER INTERESTED PERSONS:
Notice is hereby given that the Board of Directors of Sagemeadow Utility District of Harris County,
Texas, will hold a SPECIAL MEETING on MONDAY, NOVEMBER 2, 2026, at 6:30 p.m., at the Clear Brook
City Municipal Utility District Building, 11911 Blackhawk Boulevard, Houston, Texas 77089, open to the
public, outside the boundaries of the District, to consider and act upon the following matters:
1. Receive comments or questions from the public [3-minute maximum per speaker],
including presentations concerning:
a) customer invoices;
b) facilities maintenance;
c) authorize appropriate action;
2. Discuss District operations, including:
a) consider customer requests for adjustment pursuant to District Policy;
b) authorize other appropriate action;
3. Engineer’s report, including:
a) authorize preparation of plans and specifications for District projects, and
authorize advertisement of bids relating thereto;
b) consider and approve award of contracts for construction projects;
c) report on status of construction contracts, and approve pay estimates and
change orders in connection therewith;
d) report on status of emergency repairs;
e) authorize other appropriate action;
4. Office Manager’s report;
5. Discuss District Building remodel and authorize appropriate action;
6. Consider adjournment.
