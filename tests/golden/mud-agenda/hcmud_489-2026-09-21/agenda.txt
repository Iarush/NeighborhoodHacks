HARRIS COUNTY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 489
NOTICE OF PUBLIC MEETING
Notice is hereby given to all interested members of the public that the Board of Directors
of the above captioned District will hold a public meeting at 1300 Post Oak Boulevard, Suite 2500,
Houston, Harris County, Texas, 77056, said address being a meeting place of the District.
The meeting will be held on Monday, September 21, 2026, at 11:00 a.m.
The Board shall consider and discuss the following matters and take any action appropriate
with respect to such matters:
1. Public comments;
2. Review and approval of the minutes of the meeting of the Board of Directors held August
17, 2026;
3. Bookkeeper's report, including financial and investment reports and the payment of
invoices presented;
4. Review and approval of audit report for the District's fiscal year ended May 31, 2026, and
any filings in connection therewith;
5. Receive Tax Assessor-Collector report and approve payment of invoices presented and
moving of accounts to uncollectible roll;
6. Receive the Financial Advisor's recommendation concerning the District's proposed 2026
tax rate, determine intended tax rate, and authorize:
a) the required notice that the Board will consider adoption of a tax rate; and
b) the means of providing such notice;
7. Report on legal action taken by the District's delinquent tax collections attorney, authorize
foreclosure proceedings, installment agreements, and the filing of proofs of claim;
8. Operator's Report, including:
a) Monthly report;
b) Disposition of delinquent utility accounts and referral of accounts for collection;
c) Appeals of District charges and consideration of customer comments;
d) Repair and maintenance of District facilities, including:
(i) Water system flushing program;
(ii) Sanitary sewer manhole inspection and rehabilitation; and
(iii) Lift station pretreatment program;
e) Status of compliance with permits; and
f) Pending insurance and reimbursement claims;
