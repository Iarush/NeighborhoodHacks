HARRIS COUNTY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 248
NOTICE OF PUBLIC MEETING
Notice is hereby given to all interested parties that the Board of Directors of the captioned
District will hold a public meeting at 13205 Cypress North Houston Road, Cypress, Harris
County, Texas.
The meeting will be held at 11:30 a.m. on Wednesday, October 14, 2026.
The subject of the meeting is to consider and act on the following:
1. Public comments
2. Minutes of meeting held September 9, 2026
3. Patrol/camera surveillance committee report
4. Bookkeeper’s report; review of checks and invoices; payment of bills; investment
of District funds
5. Arbitrage rebate report for Series 2015 and 2016 bonds
6. Tax assessor-collector’s reports for District & Defined Area; invoices & checks;
status of delinquent taxes; authorize actions for collection of delinquent accounts,
including termination of water service
7. Public Hearing on Proposed 2026 Tax Rate
8. Order Setting Tax Rate, Approving Tax Roll, and Levying Debt Service Tax and
Maintenance & Operation Tax for the Year 2026
9. Amendment to Statement of Directors
10. Update District website to include adopted 2026 tax rate information
11. Engineer’s report: project design; approve advertisement for bids, contract award,
pay estimates, and change orders; capital improvements plan; service to 290/
Huffmeister/Hempstead Road triangle tract; letter of no objection to encroachment
12. Engage accountants for Agreed Upon Procedures Report - reimbursement of
Weiser Business Park entity for left turn lane project serving Defined Area
13. Operator’s report; repair and maintenance of District water, sewer, and drainage
facilities; customer appeals & account adjustments; termination of service for open
delinquent accounts
14. Matters related to the North Harris County Regional Water Authority
15. Detention pond operator report; authorize repairs and maintenance
16. Pending business
