HARRIS COUNTY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 284,
OF HARRIS COUNTY, TEXAS
Notice is hereby given to all interested members of the public that the Board of Directors
of the above captioned District (the "Board") will hold a regular public meeting at 19623 Windy
Stone Drive, Katy, Texas 77449, Harris County, Texas, said address being a meeting place of the
District.
The meeting will be held Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at 12:00 Noon.

or appropriate with respect to such matters:

Information Form;
