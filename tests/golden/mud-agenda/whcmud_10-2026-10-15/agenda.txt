WEST HARRIS COUNTY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 10
NOTICE OF PUBLIC MEETING
Notice is hereby given to all interested members of the public that the Board of Directors of
the captioned district will hold a public meeting at 10000 Memorial Drive, Suite 260, Houston,
Harris County, Texas. The meeting will be held at 11:00 a.m. on Thursday, October 15, 2026.
The items of business to be considered and transacted at said meeting are as follows:
1. Approve minutes of prior meeting.
2. Consider consultants reports, as needed:
a. Bookkeeper.
b. Tax Assessor-Collector.
c. Operator.
d. Engineer.
e. Financial Advisor.
f. Communications Report and any related matters.
3. Conduct public hearing on proposed 2026 tax rate.
4. Adopt order establishing ad valorem tax rate for 2026 and amend district information
form, if necessary.
5. Approve security camera access agreement.
6. Convene Executive Session pursuant to Open Meetings Act, Texas Government Code
Sec. 5 51. 071 to consider legal matters.
7. Reconvene public session and take action on any matters discussed during the
executive session.
8. Pay bills.
9. Pending business.
10. Adjournment.
