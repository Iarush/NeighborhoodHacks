ENCANTO REAL UTILITY DISTRICT
NOTICE OF PUBLIC MEETING

Notice is hereby given to all interested members of the public that the Board of Directors of the above
captioned District will hold a public meeting at Bridgestone MUD Operations & Water Education Center,
19720 Kuykendahl, Spring, Harris County, Texas, 77379, said address being a meeting place of the
District.

The meeting will be held on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, at 12:00 p.m. The Board shall act upon
the following matters:

1. Consider questions and comments from the public (the Board imposes a three-minute
speaking limit for each member of the public wishing to address the Board; comments will be
closed after this item);

2. Review and consider approval of minutes from the September 15, 2026, and September 23,
2026, Board of Directors meetings;

3. Consider report of activities of the Constable patrol in the District, and the taking of any
actions required in connection therewith;

4. Conduct a public hearing relative to the District’s proposed 2026 debt service tax rate and
maintenance tax rate;

5. Consider the adoption and levy of the District’s proposed 2026 debt service tax rate and
maintenance tax rate, and the adoption of an Order in connection therewith;

6. Consider the approval and execution of an Amendment to the Eighth Amended and Restated
District Information Form with Map Attached relative to the levy of the District’s 2026 debt
service and maintenance tax rate;

7. Consider report on the status of collection of taxes, the status of collection of delinquent tax
accounts, and the payment of invoices in connection therewith, and the taking of any actions
required in connection therewith;

8. Consider report on the District’s water, sanitary sewer and storm sewer systems, including:
a) review of customer billing;
b) review of utility operations;
c) repair and maintenance of District facilities;
d) customer appeals;
e) status of collection of backcharges, including charges assessed to GLM Contracting, Inc.;
f) status of operation of water re-use system at wastewater treatment plant;
g) status of District permits;
and the taking of any actions required in connection therewith;

9. a) Consider authorizing the design, advertisement for bids and/or award of construction
contracts for the construction of water, sanitary sewer and drainage facilities within the
District, including:

i) authorize preparation of plans and specifications for facilities to serve the 4.5339-acre
annexation tract (retail and warehouse development);

10. Consider approval of Resolution Establishing Criteria for Sureties Issuing Payment and
Performance Bonds, and the taking of any actions required in connection therewith;

11. Consider status of development of property within the District, including requests for
annexation, status of receipt of feasibility deposit, authorize engineer to prepare feasibility
study and approval of feasibility study, including:

a) status of annexation of a 13-acre site on Mossy Oaks Dr. for proposed single-family

b) review and approval of updated feasibility study for 3.33-acre tract located at 22202

12. Consider status of annexation of 4.5339-acre tract relative to proposed retail and warehouse
development, and the taking of any actions required in connection therewith;

13. Consider issuance of utility commitments and/or “will serve” letters, and the taking of any
actions required in connection therewith;

14. Consider financial and investment reports, including invoices presented, and the taking of any

actions required in connection therewith, including authorizing the payment of invoices
presented;

15. Report relative to status of District website;

16. Recess to Executive Session to discuss real property matters pursuant to §551.072, Texas
Government Code;

17. Reconvene in Open Session and authorize appropriate action related to real property matters
pursuant to §551.072, Texas Government Code;

18. Status of acquisition of Water Plant No. 2 access property, and the taking of any actions
required in connection therewith; and

19. Consider matters for possible placement on future agendas.
