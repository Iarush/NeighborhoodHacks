NOTICE OF MEETING
HARRIS COUNTY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 285
The Board of Directors of Harris County Municipal Utility District No. 285 will
hold a regular meeting on Monday, October 19, 2026, at 6:30 p.m., at the offices of Allen
Boone Humphries Robinson LLP, 3200 Southwest Freeway, Suite 2400, San Jacinto
Room, Houston, Texas, to discuss and, if appropriate, act upon the following items:
1. Hear statements from the public and receive written questions.
2. Minutes of prior meetings.
3. Financial and bookkeeping matters, including payment of the bills and
review of investments.
4. Tax assessment and collections matters, including payment of tax bills,
installment agreements, delinquent tax collections, sales tax reports, and
requests for estimates of taxable value.
5. Conduct public hearing regarding District’s tax rate, adopt Order Levying
Taxes, and authorize execution of Amendment to Information Form.
6. District website matters, including emergency alert/messaging services.
7. Security services matters, including report from Harris County Sheriff’s
Office.
8. Development in the District.
9. North Channel Water Authority report.
10. Detention facilities report, including proposals and task orders for
additional maintenance.
11. Garbage collection and recycling services, including report from Best
Trash.
12. Engineer’s report on construction projects in the District, including the
following:
a. authorize design of facilities in the service area;
b. approve plans and specifications and authorize advertisement for
bids for construction of facilities in the service area, including:
i. right turn deceleration lanes to serve Uvalde West tract; and
ii. right turn deceleration lanes to serve New Forest Town
Center tract.
c. review bids and award contracts for construction of facilities in the
service area, including:
i. water plant no. 1 drainage improvements;
ii. water, sewer, drainage and paving facilities to serve New
Forest Town Center tract; and
iii. water, sewer and drainage facilities to serve Uvalde West;
