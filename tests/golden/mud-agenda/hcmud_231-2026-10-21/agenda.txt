Taxing units such as the District adopt their tax rates under the authority of Chapter 49,
Texas Water Code and are not governed by the no-new-revenue tax rate calculations set forth in
Chapter 26, Texas Tax Code. Therefore, this Taxpayer Impact Statement does not provide a
comparison of property tax bills for the current fiscal year to be a balanced budget funded at the
no-new-revenue-tax rate as calculated under Chapter 26, Texas Tax Code.

'While the legislation uses the term “median-valued,” such term does not appear in the sections of
Texas Tax Code Chapter 26 that are applicable to taxing units such as the District. Laws appliable
to the District require is tax assessor-collector to calculate the tax rate using the “average taxable
value” of homestead properties. (Texas Water Code, Sections 49.23601, 49.23602(d) and 49.23603).
Therefore this notice calculates the property tax bill using such “average taxable value.”
