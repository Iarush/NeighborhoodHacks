EMERALD FOREST UTILITY DISTRICT
The Board of Directors of Emerald Forest Utility District will hold a regular meeting

2. Approve minutes.
3. Website and communication matters.
4. Garbage collection and recycling matters.
5. Security matters.

Taxes, and authorize execution of Amendment to Information Form.

and matters regarding termination of water and sewer service to delinquent
tax accounts, installment agreements, and placement of aged personal
property taxes on reserve list.

investments, and transfer of funds.
