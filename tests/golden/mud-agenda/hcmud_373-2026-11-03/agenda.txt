HARRIS COUNTY MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT NO. 373
NOTICE OF REGULAR MEETING

Notice is hereby given to all interested members of the public that the Board of Directors
of the above captioned District will hold a regular meeting on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at
6:30 p.m., at 3709 Coral Reef Drive, Seabrook, Texas 77586, said address being a meeting place
of the District, to consider, discuss and adopt such orders, resolutions or motions and take other
direct or indirect actions as may be necessary, convenient or desirable with respect to the matters
listed below. A copy of this Notice can be viewed on the District’s website at www.aswtax.com
(under the Public Information tab).

The subject of the meeting will be as follows:
il. Review and approve minutes of the previous meeting(s).

2 Public Comments. This provides an opportunity for the public to comment on agenda items
or other District business in advance of regular business of the Board. Comments shall be
limited to three (3) minutes per person. By state law, comments by the Board members
on any item not on the agenda shall be limited to: a) statements of specific factual
information given in response to any inquiry; b) a recitation of existing policy in response
to any inquiry; or c) a proposal to place the subject on the agenda for a future meeting.

3. Receive Bookkeeper’s report, including:

(a) approve financial and investment reports; and
(b) authorize the payment of invoices presented.

4, Receive Tax Assessor-Collector’s report, including:

(a) authorize the payment of invoices presented;

(b) receive report on the status of delinquent tax accounts;

(c) approve tax refunds and requests for waiver of delinquent penalties and interest;

(d) conduct a public hearing in connection with the levy of the District's 2026 tax rate;

(e) consider levy of the District's 2026 tax rate and the adoption of an order in
connection therewith; and

(f) approve and execute an Amended District Information Form.
5. Receive Engineer’s report.
6. Discuss park and recreational facilities.
7. Receive Attorney’s report.
8. Pending business.

9. Such other matters that may come before the Board.10. Adjournment.
