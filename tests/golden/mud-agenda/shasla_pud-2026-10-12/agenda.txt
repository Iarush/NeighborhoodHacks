SHASLA PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT
OF
HARRIS COUNTY
TO: The Board of Directors of Shasla Public Utility District of Harris County, Texas and all
other interested persons:
Notice is hereby given that the Board of Directors of Shasla Public Utility District will hold a Regular
Meeting on Monday October 12, 2026 at 2:00 p.m. at 21219 Nottinghill Dr., Spring, TX 77388 outside
the boundaries of the District, to consider and act upon the following matters:
1. District Check Signing;
2. Public Comments;
3. Consider approving the minutes of previous meetings;
4. Report of the Tax Assessor/Collector, including:
(a) Authorizing payments from the District’s tax account;
(b) Review Status of Delinquent accounts and take necessary action;
(c) Conduct Public Hearing on Proposed Tax Rate
(d) Adopt Order Establishing Ad Valorem Tax Rate for 2026
(e) Amend District Information Form
5. Engineer’s Report, including and taking necessary action on:
(a) Discuss and take necessary action on additional engineering items as listed on the
report;
6. Operator’s Report;
(a) Discuss and approve repairs within the District as necessary;
(b) Discuss any issues from residents;
(c) Amend District Rate Order as necessary;
7. Discuss and take necessary action on items at the District’s West Detention Pond;
(a) Pond Condition Report
8. District Insurance Policy Renewal;
9. Bookkeeper’s Report, including:
(a) Authorizing payment of District bills;
(b) Review Bookkeeper’s and Quarterly Investment Reports;
10. Such other matters as may come before the Board.
