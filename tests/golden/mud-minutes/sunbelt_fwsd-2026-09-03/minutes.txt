OPERATOR'S REPORT
Mr. Wright presented the Operator's Report, a copy of which is available upon request, and
reviewed the operation of the District's water and wastewater faci lities, including all necessary
maintenance and repairs that were performed over the last billing cycle.
Mr. Wright next reported the amount of revenue collected by the District during the most
recent billing cycle, which was $1,021,413.97.
Mr. Wright next reviewed with the Board the August 2026 door tag and cut-off count and
stated that 64 accounts are currently scheduled for termination for nonpayment.
Fairgreen/High Meadows Service Area ("Fairgreen/High Meadows")
Mr. Wright reported on the status of water capacity, production, and ultrasonic leak
detection in Fairgreen/High Meadows. Mr. Wright further reported that the total pumpage for the
month of August was 21,015,000 gallons.
Mr. Wright next reported that during the previous billing period 53 water service accounts
were issued door tag notices for past due payments in Fairgreen and two (2) accounts received a
second (2nd) notice that such service would be terminated; and that 248 such accounts were issued
door tag notices for past due payments in High Meadows and 25 accounts received a second (2nd)
notice that such service would be terminated.
Mr. Wright then explained that one (1 ) delinquent water account in Fairgreen was
delinquent in the amount of $33.64, and three (3) additional delinquent water accounts in High
Meadows were delinquent in the total amount of $783.56, and such accounts are 90 or more days
delinquent; therefore, MOC recommends such accounts be written off because of the age of
delinquency. A discussion ensued and it was the consensus of the Board to write off the delinquent
accounts recommended by MOC.
Mr. Wright further reported that over the previous reporting period, Fairgreen's Wastewater
Treatment Plant ("WWTP") operated at 48% of the amount of capacity allowable by the Texas
Pollutant Discharge Elimination System ("TPDES") permit and the High Meadows WWTP is
using 28% of such amount allowable by such permit.

In response to Director Garcia's question regarding funding of future District capital
improvement projects and the impact such projects would have on EAD, Mr. Barner reminded
Director Reyes that if he participates in a discussion regarding, or votes on, EAD matters, such
participation or action is considered a conflict of interest. Mr. Barner noted that because Director
Salazar and Director Garcia are appointed officials of EAD, such comments or questions create a
conflict of interest.
Mr. Barner then noted that to avoid such conflict, Director Garcia should address such
EAD matters with EAD staff and consultants, which should then be communicated to the District,
as necessary, through the appropriate channels of communication. Mr. Barner further noted that in
the event Director Salazar and Director Garcia, as directors of the Board, had a District issue
related to an EAD matter, such issue must be discussed with the District's consultants directly.
