SUNBELT FRESH WATER SUPPLY DISTRICT
NOTICE OF PUBLIC MEETING
Notice is hereby given to all interested members of the public that the Board of Directors (the
"Board") of Sunbelt Fresh Water Supply District (the "District") will meet in their regular Engineering and
Operations session, open to the public, on Thursday, September 3, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. at the District
Administration Office, located at 410 W. Gulf Bank Road, Houston, Texas 77037. Such session may be
viewed online by visiting www.sunbeltfwsd.com. The following matters may be considered and acted upon:

the public);
