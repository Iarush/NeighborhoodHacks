CONSIDER SETTING TAX RATE FOR 2026
Next, the Board discussed the proposed tax rate for the 2026 tax year (the "Tax
Rate"). Mr. Blitch submitted to and reviewed with the Board a copy of the 2026 Tax Rate
Analysis as prepared by Blitch. A copy of the 2026 Tax Rate Analysis is attached hereto
as an exhibit to these minutes. He recommended that the Board consider setting a debt
servicetaxrateof $0.266per$100of assessedvaluationforthe2026 taxyear. Mr. Blitch
then discussed with the Board the proposed 2026 maintenance tax rate for the District.
He explained that the District could levy a maintenance tax rate of $0.1309 and therefore
levy a total tax rate of up to $0.3969 per $100 of assessed valuation without the necessity
of an election by the qualified voters of the District to determine whether to approve the
adopted tax rate. ln the alternative, he continued, the District could continue to levy a debt
service tax rate of $0.266 per $100 of assessed valuation and maintenance tax rate of
$0j24 per $100 of assessed valuation for a total tax rate of $0.39 per $100 of assessed
valuation. A lengthy discussion ensued regarding the Tax Rate.
Then, upon a motion duly made and seconded, the Board voted 3 to 0 to authorize
publication of the tax rate calculation showing a debt service rate of $0.266 per $100
valuation and a maintenance tax rate of $0.124 per $100 valuation for a combined tax
rate of $0.39 per $100 valuation. The Board agreed that the tax rate hearing would be
held on Thursday, 22 October 2026.
