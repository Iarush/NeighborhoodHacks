Meetings

Our district holds two meetings per month at the Northampton MUD Building.  These meetings typically occur on the 1st and 3rd Monday of each month at 5:00 p.m., unless the date falls on a holiday.  The first meeting of each month is largely dedicated to the operation of the District’s parks and recreation facilities.  The second meeting of the month is the regular meeting, which involves all of the District’s business, including, but not limited to, the tax assessor’s report, bookkeeper’s report, operator’s report, general manager’s report, and the attorney’s report.

Public comments are welcome at our meetings.  When attending a meeting, please sign in and fill out a comment card.  The Board of Directors will address your concerns and invite comments from the public at the beginning of each meeting.

Residents of the district have the right to request the designation of a meeting location within the district under Section 49.062(g), Water Code. A description of this process can be found at (https://www.tceq.texas.gov/downloads/water-districts/forms/form-20863.pdf).

Please see the agenda for the upcoming meeting.

Location

Meetings are held at the Northampton MUD Building at 6012 Root Road, Spring, TX 77389.
