    Meetings

    The District generally meets on the fourth Tuesday of the month at 1:00 p.m. at the H2O Innovation Houston Office: 17332 Groeschke Road, Houston, Texas 77084.

    Board meetings are open to the public in accordance with Texas law. Notice of meetings will be posted within the District and on the website at least 3 business days prior to each meeting. The Board welcomes and encourages public participation and/or attendance. On occasion, special meetings may be scheduled for dates and times other than the Board's regularly scheduled meetings, as needed.
