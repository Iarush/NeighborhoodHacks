BOARD MEETING INFORMATION

The Board of Directors for Cypress Hill MUD 1 will meet each month at the Cypress Mill Recreation Center, 14710 Mills Park Lane, Cypress, Texas. Meetings are generally held at 10:00 a.m. on the third Friday of the month, and are open to the public. The Board welcomes and encourages District residents to attend.

The next regular Board meeting will be held on Friday, November 20th, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. at the Cypress Mill Recreation Center, 14710 Mills Park Lane, Cypress, Texas.
